Model and assumptions

  • Wing area S is set by the stall speed: S = 2W / (ρ Vstall² CLmax), with CLmax = factor × cl,max at Re = 300,000.
  • Untwisted wing, one airfoil, single linear taper from root to tip, section lift slope 2π, 40 lifting-line stations.
  • Cruise CL = 2W / (ρ V² S). Landing at 1.2 Vstall on a 3° path uses L = W cos γ. Landing stall check: peak cl against the airfoil cl,max (linear lifting-line cl, no stall-onset modelling).
  • Drag: HW1 component build-up for CD0 (wing, tail, fuselage; depends on t/c only) plus lifting-line induced drag.
  • Spar: solid wood, constant cross-section cantilever, sized to just meet the tip-deflection limit under the load-factor × cruise lift distribution, rounded up to whole mm. Wing weight and inertia relief are ignored. Rectangle depth ≤ (t/c) × tip chord and width ≤ the width cap × tip chord; round diameter ≤ (t/c) × tip chord.
  • αL0 for S1223 is uncertain (about −8.8° to −13° depending on method). Use the αL0 adjustment to see how sensitive a design is.
  • This is a conceptual-design tool, not a certified analysis.
