Model & assumptions

  • Wing area from the stall speed: S = 2W / (ρ Vstall² CLmax), with CLmax = factor × cl,max (XFLR5 polars, Re = 300,000, Ncrit = 9).
  • Untwisted wing, one airfoil, single linear taper, unswept quarter-chord line, section lift slope 2π, Prandtl lifting line with 40 stations.
  • Cruise CL = 2W / (ρ V² S). Landing at the landing-speed factor × Vstall on the descent angle with L = W cos γ. Landing stall check: peak lifting-line cl against the airfoil cl,max (no stall-onset modelling).
  • Drag: component build-up for CD0 (wing, tail, fuselage; depends on t/c only) plus lifting-line induced drag.
  • Spar: solid wood, constant cross-section cantilever, sized to just meet the tip-deflection limit under load factor × the cruise lift distribution, rounded up to whole mm. Wing weight and inertia relief ignored. Rectangle depth ≤ (t/c) × tip chord and width ≤ the width cap × tip chord; round diameter ≤ (t/c) × tip chord.
  • The S1223 zero-lift angle is uncertain (about −8.8° to −13° depending on the method). Use the αL0 adjustment to test sensitivity.
  • Conceptual-design tool for learning; not a certified structural or aerodynamic analysis.
